Senior Applied Scientist @ Oracle

Archita is a Senior Applied Scientist at Oracle in Hyderabad, India, where she works on agentic order-capture and advisor tooling for healthcare providers, including an Advisor Agent API built on Azure OpenAI.

Before Oracle, she spent five years at Couture.ai in Bengaluru — starting as a Data Science Intern and rising to Data Scientist 3 — building large-scale semantic search, embedding, and NLP systems for e-commerce, including a GIST-based embedding model, contrastive sentence transformers trained with DeepSpeed, and context-aware spell correction.

She holds a Master of Science in Mathematics and a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science, both from BITS Pilani, Goa.
